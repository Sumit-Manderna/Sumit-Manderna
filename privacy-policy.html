Privacy Policy

Puzzle Piece Buddy · Effective September 26, 2026

Puzzle Piece Buddy is an offline picture-puzzle game. You do not need to create an account. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The App offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob; watching an ad is voluntary and awards one in-game hint.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sumit Manderna (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Puzzle Piece Buddy mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information stored by the App

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • level progress, scores, stars, hints, and daily-puzzle history;
  • tutorial status;
  • music, sound, volume, and vibration preferences; and
  • an age category (child, teen, or adult) used to apply appropriate privacy and advertising protections.

The neutral age screen processes the entered date of birth only on the device to determine an age category. The exact birth date is not saved or transmitted. We do not receive this locally stored game information on our servers. The current version of the App does not provide user accounts, cloud saves, or direct collection of your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

2. Advertising and information handled by Google

The App uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location;
  • app and advertisement interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app or SDK performance; and
  • permitted device identifiers, such as the App Set ID, where available.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, perform analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations. Information handled by Google is governed by Google’s policies. You can review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob privacy information

Because the App includes children in its audience, advertising requests for children and users whose age is unknown are configured for child-directed treatment. The App limits advertising content to Google’s G rating, does not serve interest-based or remarketing ads to children, and removes access to the Android advertising ID.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request consent before requesting advertisements. Depending on your region and choices, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. When required in your region, you can review or change your advertising choices from Settings → Privacy Choices in the App.

You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID using your device’s privacy or advertising settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; it may change how ads are selected and how much information is used for advertising.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, advertising-related information may be processed with your consent, to provide the rewarded ad you request, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and service reliability, as permitted by applicable law.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in the consent message and Google’s policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until you reset progress, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Because the App has no user accounts and we do not receive your local game progress on a server, there is no online game account for us to delete.

Google retains advertising-related information according to its own policies and legal obligations. You may use the device and Google privacy controls described above to manage relevant identifiers and choices.

7. Security

Advertising data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children’s privacy

Puzzle Piece Buddy is designed for a general audience that includes children aged 9 and older. We do not ask children to create accounts or directly provide names, email addresses, precise locations, contacts, photos, or payment information. A neutral age screen is used to select appropriate privacy protections without retaining the entered birth date.

Advertising shown to children or users of unknown age is requested using child-directed treatment, is limited to content rated G, and is not based on interest-based advertising or remarketing. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning a child’s privacy. Local game data can be removed by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request about information controlled by us, contact us using the address below. Requests concerning information controlled by Google should be directed to Google through its privacy tools and policies.

10. International processing

Google and its service providers may process advertising-related information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
